# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: Nov 20th, 2025**

## Overview

This Chrome extension (“the Extension”) provides a customizable dark theme and visual filters (such as contrast, invert, and grayscale) for the official USACO website (https://usaco.org). It exists solely to improve readability and reduce eye strain in low-light environments.

The Extension does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data. All functionality operates locally within your browser.

## Information the Extension Collects

**The Extension does not collect or store any personal information.**

The Extension does **not** collect:

- Your name

- Your email address

- Browsing history

- Form inputs

- IP address

- Cookies

- Advertising identifiers

- Analytics data

- Any content from websites except what is needed to apply visual styles

The Extension does **not** use tracking, analytics, logging, telemetry, or any third-party data services.

## Local Processing Only

The Extension modifies the visual appearance of USACO pages by inserting CSS and applying simple filters.
This processing happens **entirely on your device** and inside your browser.

The Extension may read page elements (such as images or text colors) **only to apply the visual theme**, and this information is **never transmitted** outside your browser.

## Use of Chrome Permissions

1. `storage` (Chrome Storage)

Used only to save your dark-theme preferences (contrast, invert, grayscale, and on/off state).
These values:

Contain **no personal data**

Remain on your device or in Chrome Sync

Are not sent to any external server

2. Host Permissions (https://usaco.org/*)

Needed so the Extension can inject style rules onto USACO pages.
The Extension does not read or modify any other sites.

3. activeTab Permission

Used only when you open the extension popup to apply your chosen settings to the active USACO tab.
The Extension does not access other tabs or read page content beyond what is needed to apply styles.

## Data Sharing and Transmission

The Extension:

- **Does not send any data to external servers**

- **Does not share data with third parties**

- **Does not use or integrate with advertising networks**

- **Does not sell, rent, or distribute user data**

There is no backend server, and no information leaves the browser.

## Children’s Privacy

The Extension is designed for general audiences and does not collect any information—personal or otherwise—from users of any age.

## Changes to This Policy

If the Extension’s behavior changes in the future, this privacy policy will be updated before any new version is published to the Chrome Web Store.
